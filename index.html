ABOUT ME

An early commitment to agriculture.

My path began in Maharashtra.

RGAGRICULTURAL ROBOTICS
& EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Growing up in Maharashtra, I saw the impact of farmer suicides. By ninth grade, I had written down my goal to work in agriculture. Farm machinery, embedded systems and vision-based rice transplanting later brought me to agricultural robotics. Today, I’m pursuing a PhD at UT Knoxville, working toward practical technologies for farmers.

My experience spans mechanical design, embedded programming, sensor integration and experimental testing—from autonomous-tractor linkage design to a wireless CO₂ sensing platform.
