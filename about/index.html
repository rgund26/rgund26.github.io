ABOUT RAJESH · WHERE IT BEGAN

About Rajesh Gund

Yes, that’s my handwriting. And yes, I wanted to become an Agriculture “Management.” I was still figuring out the grammar—and the career—but I knew the purpose. I never imagined that, more than a decade later, I’d be pursuing a PhD in agricultural robotics—and still smiling at where it began.

I’m Rajesh Gund, a PhD student at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. My interest in engineering began with a concern for the farmers around me.

Growing up in Maharashtra, I saw the impact of farmer suicides. That experience drew me toward agriculture—and toward engineering as a way to contribute.

Farm-machinery training, an introduction to embedded systems at Barnstorm Agtech, and my master’s research on vision-based rice transplanting led me into agricultural robotics. I was also one of five students selected across India for a 45-day SERB-sponsored “Robotics in Agriculture” internship at SKUAST-K.

Today, I’m pursuing my PhD at UT Knoxville under Dr. Chetan Badgujar, with the goal of developing practical technologies for farmers in India and beyond.
