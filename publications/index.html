2023 · EXPERIMENTAL STUDY

Optimizing Nursery Trays for Minimizing Seedlings per Pick of Transplanter in SRI

Gund, R., Dhananchezhiyan, P., Kavitha, R., Surendrakumar, A., & Thirukumaran, K.

International Journal of Environment and Climate Change, 13(10), 2813–2824

An experimental study of nursery-tray configurations for SRI transplantation. The study evaluated seed density and soil filling height, identifying a configuration that reduced seedlings per pick and missing hills.
