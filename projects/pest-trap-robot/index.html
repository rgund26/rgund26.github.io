PHD RESEARCH · MOBILE ROBOTICS

Autonomous Pest Trap Service Robot

The Mobile Manipulator Pest Trap (MMPT) project combines deep-learning-based insect-trap detection with a mobile platform for future autonomous trap servicing in food-storage warehouses.

THE CHALLENGE

Make pest monitoring easier to service.

Insect traps in large food-storage structures need regular inspection and replacement. A mobile service robot could reduce repeated manual collection, particularly in hard-to-reach floor areas.

MY ENGINEERING WORK

Integrating perception with a mobile platform.

Robot integration

Integrated a goBILDA Recon differential-drive base, NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX 8GB and Intel RealSense D455 camera.

Data and perception

Curated and annotated a 584-image trap dataset in CVAT and trained a YOLOv8n-OBB model to estimate trap position and orientation.

Manipulation development

Developing camera-to-arm calibration and integration of a 6-DOF arm with a suction end-effector. ROS 2 and navigation integration are ongoing.

MODEL DEVELOPMENT

Detection with orientation.

Oriented bounding boxes represent a trap’s position and angle, providing information needed for future grasp alignment. Dataset split: 410 training, 117 validation and 57 test images.

PROJECT STATUS

What works. What comes next.

Demonstrated

  • Built low-profile differential-drive mobile robot integrating goBILDA Recon, Jetson Orin NX 8GB, and Intel RealSense D455
  • Curated and OBB-annotated 584 real-world trap images under varying lighting and complex backgrounds
  • Trained and benchmarked the oriented trap detector

In progress

  • Camera calibration for accurate grasping
  • Implementing ROS 2 framework across mobile platform and mobile manipulator
  • SLAM & navigation expansion with 2D LiDAR and Nav2 stack
  • Integrating 6-DOF robotic arm with dual-suction end-effector for trap manipulation

Research at UT Knoxville in collaboration with USDA-ARS Center for Grain and Animal Health Research. Project support: USDA-NIFA CPPM, award 2024-70006-42874.
