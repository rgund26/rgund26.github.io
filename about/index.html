I build across the boundaries between hardware, software and the agricultural problem.

My path began with agricultural engineering at Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth, followed by a master’s in Farm Machinery & Power Engineering at Tamil Nadu Agricultural University. That foundation connects machine design and field operations with my current work in robotics and sensing.

At CSIR–CMERI, I contributed to precision-agriculture systems through embedded programming, mechatronics, CAD and sensor troubleshooting. My earlier work at Barnstorm Agtech involved a three-point linkage for an autonomous tractor.

Today, my doctoral projects bring those skills together: an autonomous pest trap service robot for food-storage environments and a wireless CO₂ sensing system for soil-respiration research.
